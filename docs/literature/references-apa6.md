## Notes on specific entries

**Lundberg & Lee (2017).** Two published paginations exist for this paper. The *Advances in
NIPS 30* (Curran Associates) volume gives pp. 4765–4774; the ACM Digital Library version of the
same proceedings gives pp. 4768–4777. The Curran pagination is used above because it is the one
indexed by Google Scholar and used by the SHAP authors. If your department prefers the ACM
version, cite `doi:10.5555/3295222.3295230` with pp. 4768–4777 — but do not mix the two.

**Brakenridge & Anderson (2006).** Crossref carries no publication year for this chapter. The
year, editors and NATO series volume are taken from the Springer book record. Confirm the editor
list against the printed volume if your department requires editors for chapter citations.

**Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency.** Cite whichever Annual Flood Outlook year(s) you
actually use. If you use the 2021–2026 series as a whole, list each year as a separate entry
with an `a`/`b`/`c` suffix on repeated years.

**Preprints.** Grandini et al. (2020) and Jafarigol et al. (2023) remain unpublished preprints
and are marked `[Preprint]`. Do not use either as a primary methodological authority. Bagiliko
et al. (2025) was a preprint in the source document but has since been published in
*Theoretical and Applied Climatology* — the published version is cited above.
